This history lives beside the application's own data, never inside your world folders, and is not synced or uploaded anywhere.

History is append-only. A restore is recorded as a new entry, never as a rewrite, so an undo can be undone and that undo undone in turn. A "restore" that discards the branch it replaced is the one failure mode that makes a history panel unsafe to open.

Labels say what changed, not that something did. "Deleted the marker Old base", not "Updated". A panel full of the word "Updated" is a panel nobody can navigate.

The three filters compose. Date range, action, and text search narrow together rather than overriding one another, and the date picker here is the same advanced control specified in components/datepicker.html — month and year jump, range selection, presets, typed entry in the locale format or plain ISO.

What is snapshotted, and what is not, is stated. Settings, markers, profiles, capture rules and the display name are all versioned. Region files are not, because they are too large — and the confirmation gate for deleting them says so rather than leaving the user to assume history has their back.
