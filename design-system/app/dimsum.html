Ten percent of launches, drawn fresh each time. One draw per launch, never twice in the same one, and never more often than stated.

Non-blocking, auto-dismissing, and it never takes focus. It does not gate startup, does not delay the application becoming usable, and does not appear during a first run, an error path, an update, or any moment the user is mid-task. It is a small hello, not a thing to manage.

Both names are correct at every funny level. The surrounding copy takes whatever voice the funny-level setting asks for; the dish's English and Traditional Chinese names do not move.

There is no setting to switch it off, and that is deliberate. What makes an un-optable surprise polite is everything above — it never blocks, never steals focus, never interrupts. Those properties are the price of not having an off switch, so none of them is negotiable.

Reduced motion is respected. The dismiss timer stops animating and the card simply appears and later disappears. Quiet hours and do-not-disturb settings suppress it entirely.
