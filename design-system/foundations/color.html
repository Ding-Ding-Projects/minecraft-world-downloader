Tonal palettes

Roles are not invented one at a time. Each is a tone taken from one of six palettes, so a role swap stays consistent across the whole system. Tone is CIELAB L*, which is what makes tone 40 on one palette and tone 40 on another read as the same lightness.

How a light role becomes its dark counterpart. The rule is mechanical, not aesthetic: a role at tone 40 in light becomes tone 80 in dark, tone 10 becomes 90, tone 90 becomes 30. Container and content swap ends of the ramp. That is why the two themes hold their contrast without either being hand-tuned, and why adding a seventh palette later needs no new decisions.

Contrast, measured rather than asserted. Each swatch above prints its ratio against the role actually drawn on it. Every on- role clears 4.5:1 against its own container and most clear 7:1. Four roles are deliberately below that and are marked not a text pair: outline is a boundary line, outline-variant a low-contrast divider, and scrim and shadow are only ever painted at an alpha. None of the four may carry label text.

Do not copy a hex out of this page. The values are printed so a designer can check them. Implementations read var(--md-sys-color-primary) from foundations/tokens.css. A hex pasted into a component is a colour that cannot follow the theme, and it will look right in exactly the one theme it was copied from — which is the hardest kind of mistake to notice, because half the reviewers never see it.
