Progress is the only place motion must not be eased. A determinate bar maps elapsed work to distance, so an easing curve would make it lie — it would appear to race and then stall while the underlying rate was constant. Progress uses linear.

Report the real numbers beside the bar. "7,742 of 12,480 chunks" tells the user whether to wait; a bare bar does not. Where an estimate is a guess, say it is one rather than counting down confidently to a time that will be wrong.

Indeterminate means the total is genuinely unknown, not that nobody looked it up. A spinner is indistinguishable from a hang, and it is the reason people force-quit applications that were working. Switch to determinate the moment a total arrives.

Reduced motion parks both. The indeterminate bar becomes a dimmed full-width track and the ring stops spinning. Because the surrounding text already carries the state, nothing is lost by that — which is the test for whether an animation was carrying meaning it should not have been.
